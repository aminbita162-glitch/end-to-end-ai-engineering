# Restaurant AI Platform

**Status:** `Development Baseline — Phase 1`

A restaurant operations intelligence pipeline providing AI-driven operational analysis for restaurant management.

## What It Is

Phase 1 of a restaurant operations intelligence system. Development baseline established. Covers AI-assisted operational analysis for restaurant workflows. A live demo exists.

## What It Is Not

- Not Production-Ready.
- Not a hardened, fully-authenticated, or multi-tenant-isolated deployment.
- Not a fully completed multi-phase product.
- Phase 1 only — subsequent phases remain in planning or development.
- Not a general-purpose hospitality SaaS platform.

## Problem

Restaurant operations generate continuous data — orders, inventory, staffing, and customer patterns — but most operators lack the tooling to extract actionable intelligence from it in real time.

## Scope

Phase 1: production baseline covering the core restaurant operations intelligence pipeline. Additional phases planned beyond Phase 1.

## Architecture

AI-driven pipeline for restaurant operational data. Architecture documented in source repository.

## Engineering Decisions

- Phase-gated delivery: Phase 1 establishes a stable production baseline before expanding scope.
- Operations intelligence focus: prioritizes actionable signals over raw data reporting.
