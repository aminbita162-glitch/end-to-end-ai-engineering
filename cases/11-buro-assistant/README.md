# Büro Assistant

**Status:** `1.0.0 · Phase 9 closed · self-hosted · not SaaS · payment not live`

An AI-powered office assistant that analyzes emails and extracts tasks from them, reducing manual processing of workplace communications.

## What It Is

An AI office assistant focused on email analysis and automatic task extraction. Designed to reduce the manual effort of processing business email and turning communications into actionable items.

## What It Is Not

- Not a live hosted SaaS service. No verified public live URL.
- Payment is not live.
- Not a general-purpose office suite.
- Not a calendar or scheduling product.

## Problem

Office workers spend significant time reading and manually extracting tasks from email. An AI layer that reads email and surfaces tasks reduces that cognitive overhead.

## Scope

- Email analysis
- Task extraction from email content
- AI-assisted office workflow support

## Architecture

AI pipeline consuming email data and producing extracted tasks. Architecture documented in source repository.

## Engineering Decisions

- Email-first scope: focused analysis of a high-volume, high-value office communication channel.
- Task extraction as primary output: structured, actionable result rather than raw summaries.
